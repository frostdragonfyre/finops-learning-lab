# FinOps Learning Lab

Original teaching examples by [Mycal Brown, PhD](https://mycalbrown.com/) for understanding the decisions behind cloud spending.

Start with a situation, work through the numbers, and explain what the result means. These exercises use synthetic figures and require no cloud account, credentials, paid services, or software installation.

## Choose an exercise

| Lesson | Question | What you’ll practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shared cloud costs](lessons/01-shared-cloud-costs.md) | Who owns the shared bill? | Equal, usage-based, and blended allocation |
| [AI unit economics](lessons/02-ai-unit-economics.md) | Which workflow costs less per useful result? | Defining an outcome and including review effort |

Read the scenario, attempt the questions, then open the worked explanation. Use [the decision record](templates/cost-decision-record.md) to document your assumptions and the reason for your choice.

The [synthetic scenario data](data/scenarios.json) contains the same figures in a structured form for learners who want to build their own spreadsheet or notebook.

## Questions and corrections

Open an issue with the lesson name, the calculation or assumption you want to discuss, and your reasoning. Do not include real bills, credentials, or private business data in a public issue.

The figures are teaching assumptions, not current cloud or AI provider prices. A worked example illustrates a method; it does not establish that one allocation rule or workflow is universally best.
